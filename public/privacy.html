Privacy policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Rashi Shah. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The only personal information it collects is what you choose to send through the contact form.

What the contact form collects

  • Your name, email address and message - so I can read your message and reply.
  • Your browser's name and version (the "user agent") - to spot automated spam.
  • A scrambled code made from your IP address - to limit how many messages one visitor can send in a short time. Your actual IP address is never stored, and the code cannot be turned back into it.

What happens to it

  • Your message is saved in a private database (Neon Postgres, hosted in Singapore) that only I can access.
  • A copy is emailed to me through my own Gmail account, so I can reply to you directly.
  • It is never sold, shared, or used for marketing, and I will not add you to any mailing list.

How long it is kept

Messages are kept until I delete them. If you would like yours deleted, email me at rashishah0803@gmail.com and I will remove it.

Services this site relies on

  • Vercel hosts the site and, like any web host, briefly logs requests to keep it running.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • Project previews load my other projects' live websites only when you open a preview; those sites have their own privacy terms.

Google user data

The contact form uses the Gmail API with permission only to send email from my own account to myself. It cannot read, change or delete any mailbox, and it never accesses anyone else's Google data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
